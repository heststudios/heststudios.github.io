Need a hand with the plan?

Support for Pocket Caper, the single-player heist puzzle starring Luna and Atlas. Known as Çifte Plan in Turkish.

Something went wrong, or a rule is unclear? Write to Hest Studios.

heststudios@gmail.com

For a bug report, include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, the level name and what happened. A screenshot is helpful if you choose to share one. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unrelated personal information.

How do I make a character wait?

Select Luna or Atlas, then tap Wait while planning. This adds one turn at the character’s current position. Use it to let a patrol pass or coordinate the two routes.

Can I change a route?

Yes. While planning, use the step-removal control to remove the selected character’s last planned step. After a failed attempt, return to planning, adjust the routes and try again.

How do I change the language?

Open the game settings and go to Game Language. Choose English, Türkçe or the system option. The system option follows your device’s preferred languages and uses English when neither supported language is listed. Changing the language preserves progress, stars and saved routes.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress, stars, routes and preferences are stored on your device. The current game has no account or game-operated cloud save. Deleting the app can remove its local data; please contact us before reinstalling to troubleshoot a problem. Device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

The game froze or closed unexpectedly

Try closing and reopening the app, then restarting your iPhone. Check for an available game update. If the problem continues, send the level name and the steps that caused it to the support address above.

Does the game need an internet connection?

The current game can be played offline and does not contain advertising. Downloading or updating it through the App Store or TestFlight requires access to Apple’s services.

Testing through TestFlight?

You can send beta feedback through TestFlight or email us. Apple handles TestFlight crash reports and usage information under its TestFlight privacy notice.
