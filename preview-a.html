03The studio

One person. Two cities. Games with soul.

Hest Studios is a one-person independent game studio — born of Istanbul, based in Torino, est. 2026. Every game is designed, coded, drawn and shipped by the same pair of hands. No committees, no focus groups — just small games made with care.

The idea is simple: games with soul. Minibuses of Istanbul, ferries of the Bosphorus, thirty-second arcade sprints, sudoku raced live against strangers — familiar streets turned into little machines of joy.
